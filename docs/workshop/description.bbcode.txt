[h2]NOW WITH STEAM DECK INTEGRATION[/h2]
The same mod supports [b]PC and Steam Deck[/b], automatically adapting its controls and UI. [b]No separate download or resubscription required.[/b]

[b]Campaign, Battle (RTS), and Unit Possession[/b] each have dedicated controller schemes that switch automatically as you move between strategic command and soldier control.

[b]Steam Deck setup[/b]
[list]
[*]Run the [b]Windows version[/b] through Proton. Tested with [b]Proton 9.0-4[/b].
[*]Press [b]Y[/b] to enter or exit possession.
[*]Hold [b]View[/b] for the in-game controls guide.
[*]Use the [b]right trackpad[/b] for menu navigation.
[*]Labelled schemes appear as [b]Unit Possession Layout[/b] in Steam's controller settings.
[/list]

[h1]Command the army. Then join the fight.[/h1]
Select a unit, press F9, and control a soldier in your Shogun 2 battle. Fight on foot, ride with cavalry, shoot from the ranks, or lead from the front. Your army keeps fighting around you. Press F10 to return to your RTS camera.

Third person is the default. Press P to switch views, scroll to change camera distance, or hold middle mouse to orbit your soldier.

[h2]What you can do[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Choose your soldier.[/b] Zoom close, point at someone in your selected unit and press F9. Generals, officers, flag bearers and musicians use their own equipment. From higher up, a soldier is picked for you.
[*][b]Fight in melee.[/b] Follow the LMB/RMB prompt and time your click as the moving indicator reaches the green zone, using the game's combat animations.
[*][b]Aim and shoot.[/b] Use bows, guns and thrown weapons. Unit accuracy affects spread; sight markings help with projectile drop. Hold/release bow shots to vary strength.
[*][b]Command from the front.[/b] Issue move/attack orders, place formations, hold position, follow and choose fire orders.
[*][b]Use unit abilities.[/b] Select abilities or special ammunition. Aim at friendlies for targeted abilities such as Inspire. Native restrictions and cooldowns apply.
[*][b]Take part in sieges.[/b] Request an individual wall climb without ordering your formation to climb. Mount/dismount where supported.
[/list]
The HUD shows your unit, weapon, ammunition, abilities and commands. Melee timing prompts remain visible when the HUD is hidden.

[h2]Install and get started[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribe and enable this mod in Shogun 2's launcher.
[*]Launch through Steam and select a friendly unit in a single-player land battle or siege.
[*]Press F9 on PC, or Y on Deck, to possess.
[*]Press F10 on PC, or Y on Deck, to return to RTS.
[/list]
Restart after updates and enable only one possession build. Remove any older installer version before using Workshop.

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[b]Your soldier - PC[/b]
[list]
[*][b]WASD / Shift:[/b] move / run.
[*][b]RMB / LMB:[/b] ready or aim / fire. Hold and release LMB for bow strength.
[*][b]Shown LMB/RMB prompt:[/b] time your response into the green zone during melee.
[*][b]1:[/b] toggle the unit's supported melee/ranged mode.
[*][b]T / G:[/b] select / use an ability or ammunition option. Aim at friendlies for targeted abilities.
[*][b]Hold Ctrl:[/b] crouch to fire, where supported. [b]M:[/b] mount/dismount the unit.
[*][b]F near a wall:[/b] request an individual climb; press again to cancel a pending approach. [b]W / S:[/b] ascend / descend during a climb; release both to hold.
[*][b]W + Shift + LMB toward an enemy:[/b] request a personal charge. Use melee mode and release RMB. Native charge conditions and attack/defensive outcomes apply.
[/list]
[b]Your unit - PC[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Q:[/b] move/attack. Hold and drag left to right to place a formation.
[*][b]E:[/b] stop. [b]Z:[/b] hold here, facing your current view.
[*][b]C:[/b] follow you, catch up at a run, then match your pace.
[*][b]R:[/b] cycle Hold Fire, Fire at Will and Fire on Command. Fire on Command makes the unit fire when you do.
[/list]
Tap Q/Z to walk; double-tap to run. Unit orders are unavailable during locked melee animations, climbing or landing.

[b]Camera and HUD[/b]
[list]
[*][b]P:[/b] switch first/third person. [b]Mouse wheel:[/b] third-person distance. [b]Hold middle mouse:[/b] orbit.
[*][b]K on PC / tap View on Deck:[/b] while possessed, hide RTS HUD, hide both HUDs, then restore both. In RTS, toggle its HUD. Melee prompts remain available.
[/list]

[b]Steam Deck combat essentials[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Left/right stick:[/b] move/look while possessed; pan/turn in RTS. Campaign right stick turns and zooms.
[*][b]RT / LT:[/b] fire / ready while possessed; select / move or attack in campaign and RTS.
[*][b]Hold L3:[/b] run. Hold forward + L3 + RT to charge in melee mode, with LT released.
[*][b]Hold R3:[/b] switch views. [b]A / B:[/b] climb / mount-dismount. [b]X:[/b] unit melee/ranged toggle.
[*][b]Hold R1:[/b] command wheel: Follow Me, Stop, Hold Here, Fire Orders. Choose with right stick/D-pad and release R1.
[/list]
Hold View for the full controls for each mode.

[h2]Requirements and known problems[/h2]
[b]Original Windows Steam version of Shogun 2. Single-player land battles and sieges.[/b] Compatible installed-game fonts are required; English advisor audio is optional. Shogun 2, Rise of the Samurai and Fall of the Samurai weapons are recognised, but not every combination is tested. Artillery keeps the game's first-person mode.

[b]NAVAL POSSESSION DOES NOT WORK.[/b] Planned for later.
[b]MULTIPLAYER IS NOT IMPLEMENTED.[/b] Versus and campaign drop-in are under consideration.

[list]
[*]Charging and wall climbing can still fail intermittently.
[*]Enemies may ignore your soldier away from the formation.
[*]Some large Deck campaign panels shrink to fit and retain small text. Campaign/battle controller transitions can occasionally fail.
[*]Visual/gameplay bugs, clipping and crashes remain. Large Ultra-unit battles can slow down severely.
[*]The original and reviewed Bugfix engines are supported; Bugfix/Loader are not required. Other engine edits, overhauls or competing UI scripts may conflict.
[*]Bow animation/audio begins with RMB, while shot strength uses held LMB.
[/list]
